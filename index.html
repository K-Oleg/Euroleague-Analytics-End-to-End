Research Insights

Home Court Advantage: Stability or Randomness?

Goal: To evaluate the impact of "home-court advantage" and its evolution throughout the season.

Hypothesis: In the second half of the season, as tournament pressure increases, teams play more aggressively at home and win more frequently.

Key Insight: The hypothesis was partially confirmed. The overall league-wide home win rate dropped from 66% to 59%; however, top-tier teams actually improved their performance.

Euroleague Shooting Efficiency Sustainability Analysis

Goal: To investigate shifts in shooting accuracy across teams of different tiers throughout the season.

Hypothesis: Elite teams maintain or enhance their efficiency during the second half of the season, while lower-tier teams experience a decline.

Key Insight: Leaders (Top 6) showed a +3.0% increase in 2-point efficiency under pressure, while others saw a sharp decline.

Efficiency and Forecasting (PIR & Forecasting)

Goal: To identify the most efficient teams using the Performance Index Rating (PIR) and build a predictive model.

Hypothesis: PIR dynamics allow for high-accuracy forecasting of a team's average efficiency in subsequent games.

Key Insight: Time-series analysis revealed that Top 6 teams have a significantly narrower confidence interval, confirming their superior stability.

Second Chance Points — He Who Wins the Boards, Wins the Game

Goal: To analyze offensive rebounding (ORB) intensity and its correlation with final league standings.

Hypothesis: League leaders maintain their concentration on offensive rebounding despite late-season fatigue.

Key Insight: Developed a "Resilience Index." The leaders' drop-off on the glass was only -3.3% (performing better than the league average of -3.7%). This proves that discipline in securing second-chance opportunities is a critical factor for playoff success.
